Guarantees

Authorised distributors

No brokers, no grey market. Every distributor is authorised by the manufacturers it represents.

Traceability

Lot, date code and origin are kept for every part, from the distributor’s warehouse to your goods-in.

Compliance

Certificates of conformity, RoHS and REACH data available with every part.

Listed prices

Prices shown are the distributor’s, per quantity break, in your currency.

Frequently asked questions

Who sells the components?

The authorised distributors in the network. Central.Parts operates search, quoting and ordering.

How does a quote request work?

You give part number, quantity and target date. The request goes to the relevant distributors and you get an answer with a tracking reference.

Which regions do you serve?

Europe, the Americas and China, with prices in local currency.

Can I connect Central.Parts to my ERP?

Yes, through the API. Request access from the contact form.

How do I become a partner distributor?

Fill in the form and choose “Distributor”. We check your manufacturer authorisations before opening the account.
