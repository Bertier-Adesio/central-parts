1. 登录

在手机上

  1. 在微信中打开公众号「Adesio Central.Parts」。
  2. 点击菜单「供应商」→「上传库存」。
  3. 您会在对话中收到一个专属登录链接（15 分钟内有效）。点击链接，供应商中心即已登录打开。
  4. 链接过期后，再次点击菜单即可获取新链接。

在电脑上

  1. 在浏览器中打开 central.parts/depot/。
  2. 用手机微信扫描页面上的二维码，页面自动登录。

登录链接为您个人专用，请勿转发：任何人打开它都能进入您的账户。

2. 注册公司

首次登录时，填写「注册公司」表格：

  • 公司全称（与营业执照一致）；
  • 注册国家/地区：中国大陆、香港或其他；
  • 中国大陆：18 位统一社会信用代码；香港或其他：公司注册号码（5 至 30 个字符）；
  • 城市、公司类型（原厂、分销商、贸易商、现货经纪商）和报价货币。

一个微信账号只能绑定一家公司。同一公司的同事请使用各自的微信，由管理员邀请（见第 7 步）。

3. 上传营业执照

注册后，在供应商中心的「上传营业执照」卡片中，拍照或选择文件（JPG、PNG 或 PDF，最大 10 MB），然后点击「上传」。香港公司请上传商业登记证或公司注册证书。

请在网页上上传，不要发送到微信对话中。审核结果将通过微信通知您。

4. 上传库存

  1. 按模板准备库存表（见第 9 步）：CSV、XLS 或 XLSX，最大 50 MB，每行一个型号。
  2. 选择报价币种（文件中的币种列优先）。
  3. 如果这是您的完整库存，请勾选「这是完整库存表」：新表将替换之前的库存。
  4. 点击「上传」。

请至少每 7 天重新上传一次库存表：超过 7 天未更新的报价将不再向采购方展示。

5. 上传报告

几秒钟后，「上传记录」中会显示报告：新增、更新和被拒绝的行数，以及每一行被拒绝的原因（例如数量无效、价格阶梯不递增）。修改文件后重新上传即可。

6. 询单与询价

  • 询单：欧洲买家的采购申请。请在 24 小时内确认是否有货。
  • 平台询价：欧洲报价平台的询价。请在截止时间前报价或回复无货。

买方信息已匿名：Central.Parts 始终是买卖双方之间的中间方。

7. 同事

公司管理员可在「同事」中为每位同事生成邀请链接（每个链接仅限一人使用，14 天内有效），通过微信发送给同事。

8. 常见问题

登录链接已过期

再次点击菜单「供应商」→「上传库存」，即可收到新链接。

提示「此微信账号已绑定公司」

您的微信已绑定一家公司。如需为另一家公司注册，请使用另一个微信账号，或联系我们。

我的报价不见了

超过 7 天未更新的报价将自动下架。重新上传库存表即可恢复展示。

需要 Adesio 账户吗？

不需要。目前 Central.Parts 供应商中心与 Adesio 账户相互独立。

联系我们

直接在公众号「Adesio Central.Parts」中留言，或发邮件至 contact@central.parts。

Supplier guide

No password is needed for the Central.Parts supplier centre: your WeChat is your login. For now, the supplier centre is separate from Adesio accounts: you don't need an Adesio account.

1. Log in

On your phone

  1. In WeChat, open the “Adesio Central.Parts” account.
  2. Tap the menu “供应商”, then “上传库存”.
  3. You receive a personal link in the chat (valid 15 minutes). Tap it: the supplier centre opens, already logged in.
  4. If the link has expired, tap the menu again to get a new one.

On a computer

  1. Open central.parts/depot/ in your browser.
  2. Scan the QR code with WeChat on your phone. The page logs you in automatically.

Login links are personal: don't forward them. Anyone who opens one gets into your account.

2. Register your company

The first time you log in, fill in “Register your company”:

  • full company name, as on the business licence;
  • country / region of registration: Mainland China, Hong Kong or Other;
  • Mainland China: the 18-character unified social credit code; Hong Kong or Other: the company registration number (5 to 30 characters);
  • city, company type (Manufacturer, Distributor, Reseller, Broker) and quote currency.

One WeChat account can be linked to one company only. Colleagues use their own WeChat and are invited by the administrator (step 7).

3. Upload the business licence

After registering, in the card “Upload the business licence”, take a photo or choose a file (JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 10 MB), then tap “Upload”. Hong Kong companies upload their business registration or incorporation certificate.

Upload it on the page, not in the WeChat chat. You are notified of the review in WeChat.

4. Upload your stock

  1. Prepare your stock file with a template (step 9): CSV, XLS or XLSX, up to 50 MB, one part number per line.
  2. Choose the quote currency (a currency column in the file takes precedence).
  3. If the file is your complete stock, tick “This is my complete stock list”: it replaces the previous one.
  4. Tap “Upload”.

Upload your stock again at least every 7 days: offers not updated for 7 days are no longer shown to buyers.

5. Upload report

A few seconds later, the report appears under “Upload history”: lines created, updated and rejected, with the reason for each rejected line (for example invalid quantity, price breaks not increasing). Correct the file and upload it again.

6. Buyer requests and RFQs

  • Purchase requests from European buyers: confirm availability within 24 hours.
  • RFQs from European quoting platforms: quote or decline before the deadline.

Buyers are anonymous: Central.Parts always stays between buyer and supplier.

7. Colleagues

The company administrator creates an invitation link for each colleague under “Colleagues” (one person per link, valid 14 days) and sends it in WeChat.

8. Frequently asked questions

My login link has expired

Tap the menu “供应商” → “上传库存” again to receive a new one.

“This WeChat account is already linked to a company”

Your WeChat is linked to a company already. To register another company, use another WeChat account, or contact us.

My offers have disappeared

Offers not updated for 7 days are withdrawn automatically. Upload your stock file again to show them.

Do I need an Adesio account?

No. For now, the Central.Parts supplier centre is separate from Adesio accounts.

Contact

Write to the “Adesio Central.Parts” WeChat account, or e-mail contact@central.parts.
